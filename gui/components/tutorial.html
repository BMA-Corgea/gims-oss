Welcome to GIMS

How GIMS works

GIMS models your lab as a small grammar. Everything below is built from five parts of speech — define them once, then enter data and run operations against them. Pick a concept to learn, or jump straight into a workspace from the rail.

Try the live interactive demo a guided, no-setup walkthrough at gims-demo.vercel.app

The parts of speech
Noun — an entity type (Sample, Batch, Product). You define its fields + a primary ID in Noun Configure. Adjective — a descriptor/state/tag applied to a noun (status, tool, classification). Verb — an action a noun undergoes (a test, move, process), with a status workflow (buckets or linear steps + gates). Adverb — modifies how a verb is performed (parameters, references, attributes). Conjunction — overrides + result annotations that link and reconcile runs (e.g. retests).
The everyday flow
1
Configure schemas — define your nouns, verbs and modifiers in the Schemas section. Do this once per project.
2
Enter data — create/edit instances in the Noun and Verb Workbenches (single or bulk CSV/XLSX).
3
Operate — track and edit runs in the Runlog Workbench: statuses, gates & sign-offs, attachments, dumps.
4
Find & verify — explore lineage in Investigation, search everything in Deep Search, and check integrity in Audit.
Tips

Projects scope everything — pick yours from the selector on each page. Manage members and roles in Account & Roles.

Use the ? button in the header on any page to launch a guided tour of that page (where available).

The left rail is your map — it lists every workspace, grouped by what it does. Click the GIMS mark to return to the launcher.
