Not in the open-core build

You've reached a feature that ships only in the full (proprietary) edition of GIMS. The public open-core build you're running deliberately leaves three subsystems out:

  • Accounts & login — this build is single-user. There is no user-account system, no sign-in, and no sessions; every action runs as one local operator.
  • Roles & permissions — no per-project RBAC, no feature-tag scopes, and no account / role administration console.
  • Nodes Compliance (21 CFR Part 11) — no HMAC-chained audit / compliance trail, e-signatures, gate sign-off enforcement, or sealed log exports.

Want these? You're welcome to build them yourself on top of this engine — or get the full edition. Email blackmapartistry@gmail.com. The compliance layer is shown live in the Compliance Relay demo.
